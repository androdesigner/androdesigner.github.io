# Privacy Policy _Last updated: replace with your release date._ Image Optimizer ("the app") is designed to process photos entirely on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do. ## Summary - **Your photos never leave your device.** The app does not upload, transmit, or share your photos or any derived data. - **There are no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.** The only network communication is **Google AdMob**, used to show ads in the free version. - **We (the developer) do not collect personal data.** Google AdMob may collect device/advertising identifiers in order to serve ads - see [Advertising](#advertising) below. - **Buying Pro removes all ads**, so no ad requests are made. ## Information we process (on-device only) To find large photos and create smaller copies, the app reads image metadata (dimensions, format, size) from your photo library and reads the pixel data of photos you choose to compress. This processing happens entirely in the app's process on your device. The optimized copies are written to your own storage. The app keeps a small local history file containing **metadata only** - file names, sizes, dimensions, formats, and timestamps. It never stores image bytes or your photo contents. You can clear this history at any time from the History screen. ## Permissions - `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` (Android 13+) / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (Android 12 and below): to list photos and read the ones you choose. On Android 14+ you may grant access to selected photos only; the app supports that. - Photo Picker: you can always choose individual photos without granting library-wide access. - Original deletion (Pro) requires the system's own deletion-confirmation dialog. The app can only delete originals after you approve each deletion. The app also requests `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, used **solely** so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch advertisements in the free version. It does **not** request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other sensitive permission, and it never uploads your photos. ## Advertising The free version of the app displays advertisements supplied by **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner and optional rewarded videos. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and use device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, and approximate location, and may use it to personalise ads. This is handled by Google under its own policies: - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy - How Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Where required (for example, in the EEA/UK), a consent prompt is shown before personalised ads are served. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings at any time. **Buying Pro removes all ads**, which stops the app from requesting ads. Your photos are still processed entirely on-device and are never uploaded. ## Purchases The Pro upgrade is processed by **Google Play Billing**. Google Play handles the transaction; we do not receive or store your payment details. The app only reads whether you own the one-time Pro product. Google's handling of payment data is governed by Google's own privacy policy. ## Data retention and deletion - Photo metadata and local history remain on your device under your control and are removed when you clear history or uninstall the app. - No data is retained by the developer, because no data is ever transmitted to the developer. Ad-related data is handled by Google (see Advertising). ## Children The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. The developer collects no personal data. Ad serving is provided by Google AdMob as described above; if you need an ad-free, children-directed experience, contact us. ## Changes If this policy changes, the updated version will be included with the app. ## Contact Replace with your support email / developer contact before publishing.
