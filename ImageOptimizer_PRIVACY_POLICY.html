Image Optimizer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Image Optimizer (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is designed to compress and optimize photos entirely on your device. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains clearly what the app does and does not do with your information.

In short: your photos never leave your device. We do not run analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not collect your personal data. The only network activity is Google AdMob, used to show ads in the free version.

1. Summary

  • Your photos never leave your device. The app does not upload, transmit, or share your photos or any data derived from them.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The only network communication is Google AdMob, used to display advertisements in the free version.
  • We do not collect personal data. Google AdMob may process device and advertising identifiers solely to serve ads; see Advertising below.
  • Buying Pro removes all ads, so no ad requests are made.

2. Information We Process (On-Device Only)

To find large photos and create smaller copies, the app reads image metadata (such as dimensions, format, and file size) from your photo library, and reads the pixel data of the photos you choose to compress. All of this processing happens locally, within the app on your device. The optimized copies are written to your own storage.

The app keeps a small local history containing metadata only — file names, sizes, dimensions, formats, and timestamps. It never stores image contents. You can clear this history at any time from the History screen.

3. Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Android 13+) and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below): to list photos and read the ones you choose. On Android 14+ you may grant access to selected photos only, and the app supports this.
  • Photo Picker: you can always choose individual photos without granting library-wide access.
  • Original deletion (Pro): uses the system’s own deletion-confirmation dialog. The app can only remove originals after you approve each deletion.

The app also requests INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, used solely so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch advertisements in the free version. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other sensitive permission, and it never uploads your photos.

4. Advertising

The free version of the app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), including a banner and optional rewarded videos. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and use device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, and approximate location, and may use it to personalize advertising. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where required (for example, in the EEA and UK), a consent prompt is presented before personalized ads are served. You can reset or limit your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

Buying Pro removes all ads, which stops the app from requesting advertisements. Your photos continue to be processed entirely on-device and are never uploaded.

5. Purchases

The Pro upgrade is processed by Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the transaction; we do not receive or store your payment details. The app only reads whether you own the one-time Pro product. Google’s handling of payment data is governed by Google’s own privacy policy.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Photo metadata and local history remain on your device, under your control, and are removed when you clear the history or uninstall the app.
  • No data is retained by the developer, because no data is ever transmitted to the developer. Ad-related data is handled by Google, as described in Advertising.

7. Children’s Privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. The developer collects no personal data. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob as described above. If you require an ad-free, child-directed experience, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be made available with the app and at this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
