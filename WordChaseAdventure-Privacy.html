Privacy Policy

App: Word Chase Adventure · Developer: AndroDesigner Games · Effective date: October 6, 2026

AndroDesigner Games ("we", "us") publishes the Android game Word Chase Adventure (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App uses and how it is handled. We built the App to work offline and to collect as little information as possible.

1. Information we do not collect

The App has no accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or microphone access. We do not run our own servers that receive your data.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your device. This includes coins, stars, experience, unlocked characters, words learned, accuracy statistics, login streak, and your settings (language, sound on/off). This information is never sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using Google AdMob, including rewarded ads (for example, to earn an extra life) and interstitial ads between levels. Ads are not shown during active gameplay. AdMob may collect and use certain information, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Learn more in Google's policies: policies.google.com/privacy and How AdMob uses data.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Children

The App is designed to be family-friendly and suitable for learners of all ages. We configure ad requests so that ads are treated as child-directed where required and we do not knowingly use personal information from children for personalized advertising. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us using the email below.

5. Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

Where required by law, the App shows a consent form before ads are served, and you can choose whether to allow personalized ads.

6. Text-to-speech

The App can read letters and words aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. Speech is handled by that engine, which is provided by your device or its manufacturer, under its own privacy terms. The App does not record your voice.

7. Permissions

The App uses the Internet permission, which is needed only to load ads. The game itself works offline.

8. Data security and retention

Because game data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. We keep no copy of it.

9. Your choices and rights

You can delete all App data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over data held by third parties such as Google; please use their privacy tools for those requests.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with a new effective date.
