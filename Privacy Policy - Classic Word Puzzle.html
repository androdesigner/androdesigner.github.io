This policy explains what information Classic Word Puzzle ("the app") handles. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do NOT collect

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information, and we do not run our own servers that store your data.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, XP, level, streak, best times and puzzles solved) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads and optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use certain data to provide and personalise ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. We do not receive your personal information from these ads.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How AdMob uses data
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Sharing

The WhatsApp / Message / Share buttons open the app you choose with a short pre-written text about your result. Nothing is sent unless you press send in that app. We do not access your contacts or messages.

5. Permissions

Internet and network state (to load ads) and vibration (for tactile feedback). No sensitive permissions are used.

6. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

7. Data retention and your choices

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove locally stored progress, uninstall the app or clear its storage.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available in the app and on our store listing.
